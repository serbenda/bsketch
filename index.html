    Help

    Getting started

    • Choose a tool in the bar at the top, or press its key, and drag on the canvas to draw.
    • Double-click a shape to write inside it, a text to change it, or a frame to rename it.
    • Draw an arrow from one shape to another to join them: it follows them when they move. In the style panel it can be straight, with an elbow (going around the shapes in its way) or curved.
    • Your drawing is kept in this browser as you go. Save keeps a .sketch file; the menu exports PNG, SVG and PDF, and has templates and text to diagram.
    • Frames hold a part of the drawing. Present (Shift+P) shows them one by one, with your speaker notes.
    • Nothing leaves your computer: bsketch works offline and cannot send anything over the network.
    • On a Mac, use Cmd where it says Ctrl.
